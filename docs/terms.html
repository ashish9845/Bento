1What Bento is

Bento is a free (no price, no ads, no in-app purchases) offline PDF toolkit and document scanner for Android and iOS. It processes your documents entirely on your device — there is no account, no cloud and no sync. The only data that can ever leave the device is an anonymous crash report, and only if you explicitly turn on Settings → Privacy → “Send crash reports” (off by default); see the Privacy Policy for exactly what a report contains. These terms cover the app and this website. Installing or using Bento means you accept them; if you don’t, please don’t use the app.

3What the app does

Version 1.0 ships nine tools — Merge PDFs, Organize Pages, Extract Pages, Compress PDF, Image → PDF, PDF → Image, Protect PDF, Unlock PDF and native Sign PDF — plus the document scanner (auto edge detection, crop, Original / Grayscale / B&W filters, multi-page review, rename and PDF export) and a local Files browser. The app says “More tools coming soon” in the Tools tab, and features may be added, changed or removed in updates.

Outputs are sanity-checked before they reach you (page counts and a render check), but PDF processing is complex — always review a result before you print it, sign it or send it somewhere important.

4Using Bento responsibly

  • Process only files you own or are authorized to modify.
  • Use Unlock only on password-protected files you have the right to open — not to defeat someone else’s protection.
  • Don’t use the app for unlawful material or to infringe anyone’s rights.
  • Respect other people’s privacy when scanning, sharing or publishing documents.

5Signatures carry legal weight

Please read this one carefully: stamping your drawn signature onto a PDF can create a legally binding document in your jurisdiction. Check the placement and the document’s contents before you sign, keep your device and files secure, and get independent advice if the document matters — Bento can’t verify your identity or the document’s validity.

6Backups are on you

Tools like Organize, Extract, Compress and Unlock transform your files, and results land in Documents/Bento (or the folder you chose). Keep copies of important originals before processing them. Because nothing is uploaded, there is no cloud copy to restore — if you delete a file or lose the device, it’s gone. Deleting a file in Bento deletes the local copy.

7Passwords

Protect PDF secures files with AES-256 encryption. Your password is never stored and can’t be recovered by us if you forget it — store it somewhere safe, and don’t share protected files’ passwords over insecure channels.
